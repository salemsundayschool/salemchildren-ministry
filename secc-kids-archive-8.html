ABOUT US

Raising children who know and love God's Word

The Children's Ministry of Salem Evangelical Church exists to teach, disciple, and nurture children ages 5–15 in a safe, joyful space every Sunday.

Our Mission

To teach every child at Salem the truth of Scripture in a way they can understand, remember, and carry with them all week long — through lessons, memory verses, songs, and hands-on activities.

01

Sunday Lessons

Age-grouped classes every Sunday, now archived here so no child falls behind.

Photos and videos so parents can see what their children are learning.

Safe Access

Our Vision

A generation of children at Salem who grow up rooted in Scripture — able to find, revisit, and share what they've learned long after Sunday is over. This archive is our first step toward that: a simple, mobile-friendly home for every lesson we teach.

Led by our team of dedicated Sunday School teachers, in partnership with parents and church administrators.
